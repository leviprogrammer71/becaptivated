Meet the author

The curiosity
behind BEHOLD!

As a lifelong learner and science teacher of more than 25 years, Christine Beltz discovered that the more she studied God’s creation, the more her faith grew.

Her prayer is that what you learn here will ignite your curiosity, deepen your faith, and draw you into the wonder of our Creator.
