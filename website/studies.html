Science topics. Scripture. Wonder.

Each devotional stands on its own, so you can begin wherever your curiosity leads.

Study 01BEHOLD! God

What “behold” truly means and how to experience God through all our senses. Discover the power of encountering the Creator directly in the creation.

Study 02BEHOLD! The One Who Has Equipped Us Well

God has equipped us perfectly for the lives He’s called us to live. Explore how His design extends to every aspect of our being.

Entropy reveals how God actively reaches into our brokenness to restore order. From chaos to design, witness God’s restoring hand.

Study 04BEHOLD! The Taste of God’s Word

How taste and Scripture invite us to savor God’s goodness. Discover the deeper meaning of tasting and seeing that the Lord is good.

05

Study 05BEHOLD! The God of the Solution

Solution chemistry becomes a picture of how the Church comes together as one body. See the science of unity reflected in God’s design.

06

Study 06BEHOLD! God With Your Selah and Sleep

The science of rest reveals God’s design for renewal. Learn how sleep and stillness are essential to spiritual and physical restoration.

07

Study 07BEHOLD! The God of Integrity

Structural integrity in science reflects God’s faithfulness. Discover how God’s unwavering character is the foundation of all creation.

08

Study 08BEHOLD! Him With Your Bleat

How animal communication points to our own communication with God. Explore how creation speaks and how we might listen more fully.

09

Study 09BEHOLD! The God of Power

God’s immense power is displayed through universal forces we experience daily. Be amazed at the magnitude of the One who holds it all together.

10

Study 10BEHOLD! The Breath-Giver

The miracle of respiration points to the One who gives us every breath. Explore how the science of breathing reveals God’s constant provision.

11

Study 11BEHOLD! The Father

Fatherhood in nature reflects our Heavenly Father’s character. Discover how creation reveals the nature of God’s care and relationship with us.

12

Study 12BEHOLD! The One Who Has Equipped Us For Battle

The immune system parallels our spiritual armor. Learn how God protects us and equips us for the battles we face.

13

Study 13BEHOLD! The Refiner

Metallurgy’s refining process mirrors God’s refining work in our lives. Understand how pressure and heat produce purity and beauty.

14

Study 14BEHOLD! The God of the Journey

The salmon’s remarkable migration reflects the Christian walk. Witness an extraordinary journey that mirrors our own path of faith.

15

Study 15BEHOLD! The God of the Salvation Equation

Mathematical precision in God’s plan of salvation reveals His careful design. Discover the exact nature of redemption.

16

Study 16BEHOLD! The Most Pleasing Aromas

The science of smell and what it means to be a fragrance of Christ. Explore how our lives can emanate the presence of God to others.

17

Study 17BEHOLD! Jesus the Way, the Truth, the Life

Scientific truths illuminate Christ’s claims about Himself. See how creation points to Jesus in unexpected and profound ways.

18

Study 18BEHOLD! The God Who Draws Us Near

Forces of attraction in science reflect God drawing us close. Discover the irresistible pull of God’s love and presence.

19

Study 19BEHOLD! God With Your Imitation
