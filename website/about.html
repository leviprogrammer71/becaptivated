My story

Curiosity became
an act of worship.

As a child, I was endlessly curious — about animals, plants, the stars, and the “why” behind everything. That curiosity never left. It simply found a deeper purpose in my faith.

Through more than 25 years of learning and teaching science, I began to see how every discovery in God’s creation points to Him. BEHOLD! is the outpouring of that journey — an invitation for others to look closer, ask bigger questions, and be captivated by the Creator.

Christine’s journey

For more than 25 years, Christine has dedicated her career to science education, shaping the minds of countless students and helping them discover the wonder in the natural world. Her passion has always extended beyond textbooks — she believes that every detail of creation points to the hand of the Creator.

God Stops in the classroom

Out of her love for both science and her faith, Christine developed “God Stops,” brief moments woven throughout her science lessons where she would pause to point her students to the Creator. These weren’t forced religious discussions, but natural reflections on the design, order, and beauty evident in everything from photosynthesis to cellular biology to the vast cosmos. Her students found these moments transformative — a chance to see science and faith not as opposing forces, but as complementary truths.

A life built on love

Christine met her beloved husband at college 40 years ago — their first words to each other were spoken in French. They married a year later and spent 37 wonderful years building a life together. In the fall of 2022, Christine experienced an unexpected loss when her husband passed away from a blood infection. Yet from this deep season of grief, her faith and purpose have only grown stronger.

The author

Today, Christine carries forward her life’s work through writing and speaking. Published by Miramare Ponte Press, BEHOLD! Captivated by the God of Creation brings those classroom “God Stops” to a wider audience — a study devotional designed to help readers of all ages recognize God’s signature woven throughout every detail of creation.

Behind BEHOLD!

A study born from a bigger story.

BEHOLD! began as a personal journey of study and wonder, but it quickly became something more — a way to help others see God’s fingerprints in the world around them.

What started as short “God Stops” in a science classroom has grown into a three-book series for all ages, plus webinars and resources to keep the conversation going.
