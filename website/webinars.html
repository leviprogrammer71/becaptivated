Frequent questions

Do I need to have read the book to attend?

Not at all. Each webinar stands on its own. The books go deeper, but anyone curious about faith and science is welcome.

Will the webinars be recorded?

Yes — members of the webinar list receive the replay link after each live session, so you can watch whenever it suits you.

Are these webinars suitable for kids or students?

Most sessions are family-friendly and designed so parents, students, and curious kids can watch together. Each invitation notes the best age range.

How often are new webinars held?

New sessions are planned regularly throughout the year. Join the webinar list to hear about each date first.
